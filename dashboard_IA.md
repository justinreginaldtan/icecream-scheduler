# Sweet Solutions — Dashboard Information Architecture

## Purpose
Provide ice-cream shop owners and managers with an at-a-glance overview of team activity, scheduling health, and payroll progress — in a way that feels friendly, human, and quick to scan.

## Primary Questions the Dashboard Should Answer
1. Who’s working today, and are all shifts covered?
2. Do I have any pending approvals or issues?
3. How many total hours and payroll costs so far this week?
4. What recent updates or requests should I review?
5. Any positive highlights or reminders to boost morale?

## Core Sections (in priority order)
1. **Today’s Summary**
   - Open shifts / total shifts
   - Staff scheduled today
   - Hours so far
   - Quick status icons (✔ staffed, ⚠ coverage issue)

2. **Current Shifts**
   - Small cards with each employee currently clocked in/out
   - Clock-in duration + role (e.g., “Ava – Scooper, 3 h 17 m”)

3. **Pending Actions**
   - Time-off requests
   - Shift swap approvals
   - Payroll adjustments

4. **Quick Actions**
   - [Create Shift] [Approve All] [Run Payroll]

5. **Recent Activity Feed**
   - Time-off approvals, schedule changes, payroll completions

6. **Sweet Moment (Optional)**
   - “Staff of the Week” or fun shop stat (“214 scoops sold!”)

## Metrics to De-prioritize / Remove
- Total employees (belongs under Employees tab)
- Upcoming shifts (redundant with schedule view)
