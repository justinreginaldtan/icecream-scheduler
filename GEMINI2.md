# GEMINI.md — Phase 2: Premium Polish Sprint
### Goal
Evolve the Sweet Solutions dashboard into a refined, premium-grade interface that feels tactile, balanced, and visually expensive — no layout or animation rewrites, just texture, depth, and type polish.

---

## 🧭 Phase Overview
We are **not** redesigning structure or motion.  
The objective: turn the current clean baseline into a “$10 K-tier” visual showcase by refining **depth**, **lighting**, **color contrast**, **typography**, and **brand zones**.

---

## 🎨 Prompt 1 — Layer Depth & Lighting
> Add realistic depth between surfaces.  
> Keep cards pure white and lift them softly off the warm-cream background using airy shadows (like Notion).  
> Sidebar remains flat mint-tinted, but distinguish it slightly with a thin vertical separator or light gradient.  
> The goal is a natural sense of elevation and breathing space without clutter.

---

## 🧁 Prompt 2 — Color Balance & Accents
> Balance the Mint-Berry palette for warmth and energy.  
> Use berry only for primary CTAs (e.g., “Create Shift”) and mint for supportive or success states.  
> Adjust background-to-card contrast so each zone feels distinct yet cohesive.  
> Ensure all success, info, and alert tokens follow the same pastel-to-rich saturation logic.

---

## ✍️ Prompt 3 — Typography & Hierarchy
> Refine the typography system for professionalism and readability.  
> Introduce subtle visual hierarchy: section headers slightly larger and darker charcoal; body text lighter.  
> Consider pairing a friendly serif (Recoleta, Fraunces, or similar) for headings with a neutral sans (Inter, Work Sans) for content.  
> Maintain consistent spacing rhythm and line-height for visual calm.

---

## 🪞 Prompt 4 — Sidebar & Brand Cohesion
> Polish the sidebar into a tactile, brand-anchored element.  
> Apply a faint cream-mint gradient or soft shadow so it feels like a physical panel.  
> Keep icons monochrome and refine spacing for alignment precision.  
> Optionally, add a tiny visual signature (heart, sprinkle, or cone icon) near the logo to humanize the brand.

---

## 🏁 Phase Completion Checklist
- Cards and background read as **distinct surfaces**.  
- Typography hierarchy is **clearly readable and consistent**.  
- Accents feel **balanced** (berry = energy, mint = calm).  
- Sidebar anchors the brand without visual heaviness.  
- No hidden or invisible hover/motion changes — focus purely on visible, static refinement.

---

**End Goal:**  
A screenshot-ready dashboard that visually communicates “crafted by a designer,” not “generated by a template.”
