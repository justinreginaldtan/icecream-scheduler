# GEMINI_CONTRAST_TUNING.md — Contrast & Depth Polish
### Objective
Improve visual hierarchy and button visibility across the Sweet Solutions dashboard.  
Buttons and surfaces should feel tactile, layered, and easy to distinguish — while maintaining the Refined Whimsy pastel warmth.

---

## 🧭 Prompt 1 — Audit
> Identify low-contrast elements where components visually blend into the background.  
> Typical areas: secondary buttons, cards, dividers, and hover states.  
> Ask the user to confirm which components should stay subtle versus pop slightly (e.g., should “Approve All” be a neutral secondary or a clear call-to-action?).

---

## 🪞 Prompt 2 — Apply Micro-Contrast Adjustments
> Refine the following specifically:
> - Secondary buttons (Approve All, Run Payroll): slightly darker neutral fill or a thin subtle outline using `--charcoal-200` or `--cream-300`.  
> - Card and section borders: increase contrast by 3-5 % relative to background.  
> - Shadows: deepen elevation for cards and buttons (1 px soft shadow, warm-tinted).  
> - Hover: ensure a clear 5–7 % tonal shift for all interactive components.  
> Maintain your current color harmony (Mint–Berry–Cream).

---

## 🧠 Prompt 3 — Verify Hierarchy
> Confirm that at first glance, the user can easily distinguish:
> - Primary vs. Secondary buttons  
> - Cards vs. Background  
> - Interactive vs. Static elements  
> Ask the user to visually confirm before finalizing changes.

---

## ⚠️ Rules
- No new colors — only tonal adjustments from existing variables.  
- No animations or glow effects.  
- Keep the soft, premium warmth of the design.  
- Ask before globally modifying variables.

---

### ✅ Deliverable
A visually balanced dashboard where interactive elements clearly stand out, preserving the refined-whimsy style.
