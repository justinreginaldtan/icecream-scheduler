# Sweet Solutions — Refined Whimsy Overhaul

## Design Intent
Warm, premium, ice-cream-shop aesthetic. Maintain friendliness while improving depth, hierarchy, and brand identity.

## Visual Priorities
1. Layered surface hierarchy
2. Strong single accent color
3. Typography rhythm and shadow depth
4. Warm cream base tone
5. Playful motion cues

## Commit Checklist
- [ ] Update global tokens (`--background`, `--surface-1`, `--surface-2`, `--accent`)
- [ ] Apply surface gradient or tinted base
- [ ] Standardize shadows and corner radius
- [ ] Reinforce heading font weights
- [ ] Add Framer Motion entry for dashboard cards

## Adjustment Notes
- “Refined Whimsy” = playful *and* premium. Do not over-restrict color or depth.
- You are allowed to visibly introduce contrast and accent vibrancy.
- Berry accent (#FF7A5C) must appear clearly on primary CTAs, hover states, and key icons.
- Interactivity should feel tactile and joyful, not sterile.
